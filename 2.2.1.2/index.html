In this understanding, food was first concocted in the stomach and intestines, producing chyle, which was then secondarily concocted in the liver into nutritive blood, then sent via the veins to the other parts of the body to be refined into humours. The third concoction involved the secretion and excretion of various substances, such as sweat, hair and tears, from various parts of the body.
