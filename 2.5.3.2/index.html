SUBSECT. 2.

Correctors to expel wind. Against costiveness, &c.

In this kind of melancholy one of the most offensive symptoms is wind, which as in the other species, so in this, hath great need to be corrected and expelled.

morechaste tree Notes Agnus castus is the standard herbal name for Vitex agnus-castus, the chaste tree, a shrub whose seeds and leaves were used in medicine. The name is a doubled 'chaste'. The Greek name 'agnos' was confused with Greek 'hagnos' ('chaste') and with Latin 'agnus' ('lamb'), and Latin 'castus' ('chaste') was then added. In English the name was often kept as 'Agnus Castus'.
trans. Electuary of bay-berries, laxative Benedicta, the powder against wind from the Florentine Antidotary, carminative powder, rose aromatic
moreNotes These are names of standard compound preparations in the pharmacopoeias. Benedicta laxativa was a well-known purging electuary. 'Antid. Florent.' is almost certainly the Antidotarium Florentinum (the Florentine pharmacopoeia), the source of the recipe. Aromaticum rosatum was a spiced powder made with roses. Some names could also be left in Latin, as the titles of recipes.
trans. nature abhors a vacuum g
morenature abhors a vacuum. djNature does not admit a vacuum. Notes A proverbial scholastic axiom (nature abhors a vacuum). Here it is applied jokingly to the bellows remedy.
