Division of Perturbations.

Perturbations and passions, which trouble the fantasy, though they dwell between the confines of Sense and Reason, yet they rather follow Sense than Reason, because they are drowned in corporeal organs of Sense. The Thomists subdivide them into eleven, six in the Coveting, and five in the Invading. Aristotle reduceth all to Pleasure and Pain; Plato to Love and Hatred; bb note 3 de Anima.Vives to Good and Bad.
