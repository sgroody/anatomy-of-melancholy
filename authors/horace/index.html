Who they are

Machine draft by claude-opus-5-5 (prompt authors-v1, 2026-11-02); accepted by the owner (2026-11-02).

Quintus Horatius Flaccus (65–8 BC) was a Roman poet from Venusia in southern Italy. His works are the Odes, the Satires (Sermones), the Epistles and the Ars Poetica.

Why Burton cites them: Burton quotes him often, mostly from the Satires, Odes and Epistles, for moral comments on greed, discontent, folly and how to live calmly.
