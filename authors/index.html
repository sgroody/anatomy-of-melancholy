        1. 3.2.1.1 Heroical love causing Melancholy. His Pedigree, Power, and Extent.
        2. 3.2.1.2 How love tyrannizeth over men. Love, or Heroical melancholy his definition, part affected.
      2. Member 2
        1. 3.2.2.1 Causes of Heroical love, Temperature, full Diet, Idleness, Place, Climate, &c.
        2. 3.2.2.2 Other causes of Love-Melancholy, Sight, Beauty from the face, eyes, other parts, and how it pierceth.
        3. 3.2.2.3 Artificial allurements of love, causes and provocations to lust; gestures, Clothes, Dower, &c.
        4. 3.2.2.4 Importunity and opportunity of time, place, conference, discourse, singing, dancing, music, amorous tales, objects, kissing, familiarity, tokens, presents, bribes, promises, protestations, tears, &c.
        5. 3.2.2.5 Bawds, Philtres, causes.
      3. Member 3
        1. 3.2.3.1 Symptoms or signs of Love-Melancholy, in Body, Mind, good, bad, &c.
      4. Member 4
        1. 3.2.4.1 Prognostics of Love-Melancholy.
      5. Member 5
        1. 3.2.5.1 Cure of Love-Melancholy, by Labour, Diet, Physic, Fasting, &c.
        2. 3.2.5.2 Withstand the beginnings, avoid occasions, change his place: fair and foul means, contrary passions, with witty inventions: to bring in another, and discommend the former.
        3. 3.2.5.3 By counsel and persuasion, foulness of the fact, men’s, women’s faults, miseries of marriage, events of lust, &c.
        4. 3.2.5.4 Philtres, Magical, and Poetical cures.
        5. 3.2.5.5 The last and best cure of Love-Melancholy, is, to let them have their desire.

      1. Member 1
        1. 3.3.1.1 JEALOUSY.
        2. 3.3.1.2 Causes of Jealousy. Who are most apt. Idleness, Melancholy, Impotency, long absence, beauty, wantonness, naught themselves. Allurements from time, place, persons, bad usage, Causes.
      2. Member 2
        1. 3.3.2.1 Symptoms of Jealousy, fear, sorrow, suspicion, strange actions, gestures, outrages, locking up, oaths, trials, laws, &c.
      3. Member 3
        1. 3.3.3.1 Prognostics of Jealousy, Despair, Madness, to make away themselves and others.
      4. Member 4
        1. 3.3.4.1 Cure of Jealousy: by avoiding occasions, not to be idle: of good counsel: to contemn it, not to watch or lock them up: to dissemble it, &c.
        2. 3.3.4.2 By prevention before, or after marriage, Plato’s community, marry a Courtesan, Philtres, Stews, to marry one equal in years, fortunes, of a good family, education, good place, to use them well, &c.
    4. Section 4
      1. Member 1
        1. 3.4.1.1 Religious Melancholy.
        2. 3.4.1.2 Causes of Religious melancholy. From the Devil by miracles, apparitions, oracles. His instruments or factors, politicians, Priests, Impostors, Heretics, blind guides. In them simplicity, fear, blind zeal, ignorance, solitariness, curiosity, pride, vainglory, presumption, &c. His engines, fasting, solitariness, hope, fear, &c.
        3. 3.4.1.3 Symptoms general, love to their own sect, hate of all other Religions, obstinacy, peevishness, ready to undergo any danger or cross for it; Martyrs, blind zeal, blind obedience, fastings, vows, belief of incredibilities, impossibilities: Particular of Gentiles, Mahometans, Jews, Christians; and in them, Heretics old and new, Schismatics, Schoolmen, Prophets, Enthusiasts, &c.
        4. 3.4.1.4 Prognostics of Religious Melancholy.
        5. 3.4.1.5 Cure of Religious Melancholy.
      2. Member 2
        1. 3.4.2.1 Religious Melancholy in defect; parties affected, Epicures, Atheists, Hypocrites, worldly secure, Carnalists, all impious persons, Impenitent sinners, &c.
        2. 3.4.2.2 Despair.
        3. 3.4.2.3 Causes of despair, the Devil, melancholy, meditation, Distrust, weakness of faith, rigid Ministers, misunderstanding Scriptures, guilty consciences, &c.
        4. 3.4.2.4 Symptoms of Despair, Fear, Sorrow, Suspicion, Anxiety, Horror of conscience, fearful dreams and visions.
        5. 3.4.2.5 Prognostics of Despair, Atheism, Blasphemy, violent death, &c.
        6. 3.4.2.6 Cure of Despair by Physic, good counsel, comforts, &c.
