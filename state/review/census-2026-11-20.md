The method and pass condition are in `data/eval/CENSUS.md`, fixed before measuring (D13). The pilot is in
`census-pilot-2026-11-20.md`. Machine numbers: `state/census/report-before.json` (the edition as the census found

V5 passes. `burton check --all` gates it on the census and reports the held-out rounds h1–h7 alongside.

## What the judge found (words)

* **Agreed:** both detectors tagged 51,837 words. On the 300 sampled agreed stretches, 2,260 of 2,262 words were
  foreign (0.9991).
* **Disagreed:** 1,897 words were judged foreign.
  * The edition alone had 189 of them.
  * Detector 2 alone had 1,708.
  * The edition also tagged 892 words that the judge ruled not foreign.
* **Missed by both:** about 6.2 words (Chapman). Both detectors are Claude models, so this is a lower bound (CENSUS.md
  step 5).

## The fixes

The judge's rulings were written to `data/layers/langid-census.json`: 750 rulings, 365 adding a passage and 385
narrowing one. Each is labelled with model, prompt and date. Ingest applies them as markup only, with
`resp="#model"`; the text is unchanged (V1 passes, 0 unexplained differences). 127 low-confidence rulings are not
applied, and they count against the edition in the score (CENSUS.md step 3).

* 605 were applied.
* 18 were not placed, and their blocks were left exactly as they were:
  * 9: no passage overlaps the stretch;
  * 5: the passage is the editor's, Chaucer, or Greek/Hebrew script, which a ruling may not narrow;
  * 4: the ruling would nest a passage or cross a verse line.

The edition's foreign passages: 7,694 at the last committed report, 7,865 now. All 7,865 have a translation record,
none stale. 281 translation records keyed by passage ids that the narrowing retired were moved to
`data/layers/retired/translations/`. Self-translation signals, the Dell & Jordan-Smith alignment and source
verification were re-run. Verified sources went from 420 to 426, because the narrowed passages match the corpora
better.

## The human cross-check (CENSUS.md step 7, not part of the gate)

Gowland's brackets mark foreign text by a human hand. 4,266 of his 4,351 translation brackets (98.0%) follow a
tagged passage, and 85 do not.

## What this result rests on

* **The fixes measure partly by construction.** The judge ruled every disagreement, and the fixes apply those
  rulings, so the "after" score is near 1 on the disagreements by design. What it does measure independently:
  * the agreed region, through the 300-stretch sample;
  * the low-confidence rulings, counted against the edition;
  * the rulings that could not be placed;
  * what both detectors miss.

  Its real assumption is that the judge (Opus, blind to which detector marked a stretch) is right. No human checked
  the judge, because the owner cannot hand-check. The pilot's look at the judge's reasons, and Gowland's 98% above,
  are the only outside checks.
* **The capture-recapture estimate assumes independent misses.** Both detectors are Claude models, so the estimate
  is a lower bound.
* **The held-out rounds still run as a regression report.** h7 now scores P 0.989, R 0.968, B 0.989; on the run
  that drew it, it scored P 0.958, R 0.968, B 0.967.
