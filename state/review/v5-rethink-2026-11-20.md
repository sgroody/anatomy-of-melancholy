# V5 rethought (2026-11-20, after the fifth cycle)

Owner: "Think critically and approach the problem from a novel direction." Five cycles have tuned the detector on one
300-item round and gated it on the next. The scores have stayed within P 0.95-0.99 and R 0.94-0.97. These are
measurements of the gate and the gold, not of the detector. No model calls were made for them.
(Script: scratchpad power.py, raters.py, weight.py.)

## 1. One round cannot resolve the targets

h7 has 95 gold positives and 96 detections. To pass, it may have at most 1 false positive, 2 misses and 4 inexact
boundaries. Binomial chance of passing all three at once, for a detector whose true rates are:

| true P / R / B | passes P | passes R | passes B | passes all three |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.98 / 0.97 / 0.95 (exactly the targets) | 0.43 | 0.45 | 0.51 | **0.10** |
| 0.99 / 0.98 / 0.96 | 0.75 | 0.70 | 0.68 | 0.36 |
| 0.995 / 0.99 / 0.97 | 0.92 | 0.93 | 0.85 | 0.72 |

A detector that meets the targets fails nine rounds in ten. The 90% intervals on h7 contain the targets: precision
92/96 gives [0.910, 0.981] and recall 92/95 gives [0.924, 0.987]. To measure recall to ±0.01 needs about 790 positives,
which is a round of about 2,400 items.

## 2. The gold is not independent of the detector

On whether an item is foreign, the settled gold agrees with the Opus rater on every item of h6 and h7 (item P 1.000,
R 1.000). Every settlement of foreignness sided with Opus, and the settler is Claude, the same model. The detector's
rulings (la-v2) are Opus too, so any error the two share cannot show. Sonnet, the second blind rater, scores against
the same gold:

| round | Sonnet P | Sonnet R |
|---|---|---|
| h6 | 0.990 | 0.970 |
| h7 | 0.969 | 1.000 |

Sonnet would not pass the targets on h7 either. The targets sit where two strong labellers already disagree. On the
mixed items' foreign parts, the two raters differed on 8 items in h6 and 2 in h7.

## 3. What the errors cost a reader

Counted by words rather than items (pooled h5-h7), the detector misses 28 of 1,665 foreign words. That is a word
recall of 0.983 (h7: 0.995). Item recall is 0.956. The misses are single words: "Rusticus", "Indae", "dei". h7's four
false positives tag 7 words in all ("cap", "Ter", "Phoebades", "Tully pro Archia Poeta"). The scorer counts the whole
English item "Hear Tully pro Archia Poeta: …" (18 words), not the 4 words that were tagged.

## 4. A census instead of a sample

- **Gowland's brackets, the whole book, at no cost.** He is a human scholar who translated nearly everything he
  judged foreign. 96 of his 4,345 brackets have no passage before them (2.2%), and the ingest report lists them all.
  Most are real misses: proverbs and short Latin clauses. Some are titles, which are citations by convention. Ruling on
  the text before each bracket uses his finding (where a bracket stands), never his wording. That is allowed
  (CLAUDE.md: his findings are facts; his wording never enters a layer or a prompt).
- **A second, independent detector over the whole book.** Use a different model (Sonnet) and a different method:
  whole blocks, marking spans, never seeing our tags. Every disagreement goes to a judge. This fixes errors everywhere
  instead of estimating them. Capture-recapture on the two detectors estimates how many errors both still miss,
  book-wide, with an interval.
- **The gate, which is the owner's decision under D10.** Options: a book-wide estimate with its interval in place of a
  300-item round; the same targets on a pooled round large enough to resolve them; or a target counted in words.
