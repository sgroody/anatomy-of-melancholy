Most of the foreign text is Latin, so its colour matters most. Each option in light and dark, beside the rubric of the note letters and initials (which must stay distinct from it).

F3The synopses on the home page (N3 again)

The front page holds the engraving on the left and the way in on the right. Burton’s synopses are printed sheets of braces read left to right, in small type. Three designs that take from both: the sheet’s own braces; the engraving’s framed compartments; and a plain list given true braces. Partition 1, real data; click to open branches.

A · the sheet, live: true braces, left to right; a click makes a branch the root

Like the printed sheet, but only three levels at a time: clicking a branch brings it to the left edge with its own braces; the trail above returns. Fits the right-hand column of the front page.

B · three compartments, as in the engraving

The three Partitions as framed panels, like the engraving’s, each with its first branches in braces; a panel opens to its full tree.

C · the outline, with true braces

Top to bottom for easy reading, but each group held by a drawn brace as on the sheet, the heads in small capitals.
