Mockups for your 3 November notes, built with Burton’s own text from 1.1.1.1 and its real notes and translations. Hover, click and pin work as they would in the edition. For each test, pick A, B (or C), or Neither, and add a note if you want. Then use Copy my choices at the bottom and paste the result back to me. Everything shown is the private build, with Gowland as its base: his translations come first, then Dell & Jordan-Smith, and Claude’s only where neither exists. His glosses and explanatory notes sit in the margin, not in the line. Every note uses lettered markers (a, b, c, as Gowland letters them), and the model is shown as “Claude”, with the full label behind the ◇ icon.
