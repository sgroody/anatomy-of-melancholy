MEMB. 2.

SUBSECT. 1.

Bad diet a cause. Substance. Quality of meats.

According to my proposed method, having opened hitherto these secondary causes, which are inbred with us: I must now proceed to the outward and adventitious, which happen unto us after we are born. And those are either Evident, Remote; or inward, Antecedent, and the nearest: Continent causes some call them. These outward, remote, precedent causes are subdivided again, into necessary and not necessary. Necessary (because we cannot avoid them, but they will alter us, as they are used, or abused) are those six non-natural things, so much spoken of amongst Physicians, which are principal causes of this disease.
