SUBSECT. 4.

Sorrow a cause of Melancholy.

In this Catalogue of Passions, which so much torment the Soul of man, and cause this malady (for I will briefly speak of them all, and in their order), the first place in this Irascible appetite, may justly be challenged by Sorrow.
