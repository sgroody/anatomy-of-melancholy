SUBSECT. 2.

Simples proper to Melancholy, Against Exotic Simples.

Medicines properly applied to Melancholy, are either Simple or Compound. Simples are Alterative or Purgative. Alteratives are such as correct, strengthen nature, alter, anyway hinder or resist the disease; and they be herbs, stones, minerals, &c., all proper to this humour.
