A ‘temperate’ constitution here means one in which government is subject to civil law, or moderated by some other constraint, and typically contrasted with ‘absolute’ rule, which is above the law. The ‘mixed constitution’, influentially theorized in antiquity by the Greek author Polybius and most famously exemplified by Sparta and the Roman Republic, is one which includes some combination of institutions that have a monarchical, aristocratic or popular character.
