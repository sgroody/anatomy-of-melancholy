MEMB. 1. SUBSECT. 1.

JEALOUSY.

Jealousy, its Equivocations, Name, Definition, Extent, several kinds; of Princes, Parents, Friends. In Beasts, Men: before marriage, as Corrivals; or after, as in this place.

Valescus de Taranta cap. de Melanchol., Aelian Montaltus, Felix Platerus, Guainerius§, put Jealousy for a cause of Melancholy, others for a Symptom; because melancholy persons amongst these passions and perturbations of the mind, are most obnoxious to it. But methinks for the latitude it hath, and that prerogative above other ordinary symptoms, it ought to be treated of as a Species apart, being of so great and eminent note, so furious a passion, and almost of as great extent as Love itself, as aa note In his Oration of JealousyBenedetto Varchi, Lezione sulla gelosia (Oration of Jealousy), published by Francesco Sansovino , put out by Fran.
