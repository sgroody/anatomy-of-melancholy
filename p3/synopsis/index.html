  • Love and Love-Melancholy, Memb. 1 Sect. 1. to 3.1.1
    • Preface or Introduction. Subsect. 1. to 3.1.1.1
    • Love’s definition, Pedigree, Object, Fair, Amiable, Gracious and pleasant, from which comes Beauty, Grace, which all desire and love, parts affected.
    • Division or kinds Subsect. 2. to 3.1.1.2
      • Natural, in things without life, as love and hatred of elements; and with life, as vegetal, vine and elm, sympathy, antipathy, &c.
      • Sensible, as of Beasts, for pleasure, preservation of kind, mutual agreement, custom, bringing up together, &c.

      • or

        Rational.
        • Simple which hath 3 objects, as Memb. 2. to 3.1.2
          • Profitable. Subsect. 1. to 3.1.2.1
            • Health, wealth, honour, we love our benefactors: nothing so amiable as profit, or that which hath a show of commodity.
          • Pleasant. Subsect. 2. to 3.1.2.2
            • Things without life, made by art, pictures, sports, games, sensible objects, as hawks, hounds, horses. Or men themselves for similitude of manners, natural affection, as to friends, children, kinsmen, &c., for glory, such as commend us.
            • Of women, as
              • Before marriage, as Heroical Mel., Sect. 2, vide go to the branch marked ♈ to 3.2
              • Or after marriage, as Jealousy, Sect. 3, vide go to the branch marked ♉ to 3.3
          • Honest. Subsect. 3. to 3.1.2.3
            • Fucate in show, by some error or hypocrisy; some seem and are not; or truly for virtue, honesty, good parts, learning, eloquence, &c.

        • or

          Mixed of all three which extends to Memb. 3. to 3.1.3
          • Common good, our neighbour, country, friends, which is charity; the defect of which is cause of much discontent and Melancholy.

          • or

            God, Sect. 4. to 3.4
            • In excess, vide go to the branch marked ♊
            • In defect, vide go to the branch marked ♋
  • ♈Heroical or Love-Melancholy, in which consider, Sect. 2. to 3.2
    • Memb. 1.His pedigree, power, extent to vegetals and sensible creatures, as well as men, to spirits, devils, &c. to 3.2.1
    • His name, definition, object, part affected, tyranny.
    • Causes Memb. 2. to 3.2.2
      • Stars, temperature, full diet, place, country, clime, condition, idleness. Subsect. 1. to 3.2.2.1
      • Natural allurements, and causes of love, as beauty, its praise, how it allureth. Comeliness, grace, resulting from the whole, or some parts, as face, eyes, hair, hands, &c., Subsect. 2. to 3.2.2.2
      • Artificial allurements, and provocations of lust and love, gestures, apparel, dowry, money, &c.
      • Quaest. Whether beauty owe more to Art or Nature? Subsect. 3. to 3.2.2.3
      • Opportunity of time and place, conference, discourse, music, singing, dancing, amorous tales, lascivious objects, familiarity, gifts, promises, &c., Subsect. 4. to 3.2.2.4
      • Bawds and Philtres. Subsect. 5. to 3.2.2.5
    • Symptoms or signs Memb. 3. to 3.2.3
      • Of Body
        • Dryness, paleness, leanness, waking, sighing, &c.
        • Quaest. An detur pulsus amatorius?

      • or

        Of Mind
        • Bad, as
          • Fear, sorrow, suspicion, anxiety, &c.
          • An hell, torment, fire, blindness, &c.
          • Dotage, slavery, neglect of business.

        • or

          Good, as
          • Spruceness, neatness, courage, aptness to learn music, singing, dancing, poetry, &c.
    • Prognostics; Despair, Madness, Frenzy, Death, Memb. 4. to 3.2.4
    • Cures Memb. 5. to 3.2.5
      • By labour, diet, Physic, abstinence, Subsect. 1. to 3.2.5.1
      • To withstand the beginnings, avoid occasions, fair and foul means, change of place, contrary passion, witty inventions, discommend the former, bring in another, Subsect. 2. to 3.2.5.2
      • By good counsel, persuasion, from future miseries, inconveniences, &c., Subsect. 3. to 3.2.5.3
      • By Philtres, magical, and poetical cures, Subsect. 4, to let them have their desire disputed pro and con. Impediments removed, reasons for it, Subsect. 5. to 3.2.5.4 to 3.2.5.5
  • ♉Jealousy. Sect. 3. to 3.3
    • His name, definition, extent, power, tyranny, Memb. 1. to 3.3.1
    • Division, Equivocations, kinds. Subsect. 1. to 3.3.1.1
      • Improper
        • To many beasts; as Swans, Cocks, Bulls.
        • To Kings and Princes, of their subjects, successors.
        • To friends, parents, tutors over their children, or otherwise.

      • or

        Proper
        • Before marriage, corrivals, &c.
        • After, as in this place our present subject.
    • Causes Subsect. 2. to 3.3.1.2
      • In the parties themselves,
        • Idleness, impotency in one party, melancholy, long absence.
        • They have been naught themselves. Hard usage, unkindness, wantonness, Inequality of years, persons, fortunes, &c.

      • or

        From others
        • Outward enticements and provocations of others.
    • Symptoms, Memb. 2. to 3.3.2
      • Fear, sorrow, suspicion, anguish of mind, strange actions, gestures, looks, speeches, locking up, outrages, severe laws, prodigious trials, &c.
    • Prognostics Memb. 3. to 3.3.3
      • Despair, madness, to make away themselves, and others.
    • Cures Memb. 4. to 3.3.4
      • By avoiding occasions, always busy, never to be idle.
      • By good counsel, advice of friends, to contemn or dissemble it. Subsect. 1. to 3.3.4.1
      • By prevention before marriage. Plato’s communion.
      • To marry such as are equal in years, birth, fortunes, beauty, of like conditions, &c. Of a good family, good education. To use them well. Subsect. 2. to 3.3.4.2
  • ♊Religious melancholy. Sect. 4. to 3.4
    • In excess of such as do, that which is not required. Memb. 1. to 3.4.1
      • A proof that there is such a species of Melancholy, Name, Object God, what his beauty is, how it allureth, Part and parties affected, superstitious, Idolaters, Prophets, Heretics, &c., Subsect. 1. to 3.4.1.1
      • Causes Subsect. 2. to 3.4.1.2
        • From others
          • The devil’s allurements, false miracles, Priests for their gain. Politicians to keep men in obedience, Bad instructors, blind Guides.

        • Or

          from themselves
          • Simplicity, fear, ignorance, solitariness, Melancholy, curiosity, pride, vainglory, decayed Image of God.
      • Symptoms Subsect. 3. to 3.4.1.3
        • General
          • Zeal without knowledge, obstinacy, superstition, strange devotion, stupidity, confidence, stiff defence of their tenets, mutual love & hate of other sects, belief of incredibilities, impossibilities.

        • Or

          Particular.
          • Of Heretics, pride, contumacy, contempt of others, wilfulness, vainglory, singularity, prodigious paradoxes.
          • In superstitious, blind zeal, obedience, strange works, fasting, sacrifices, oblations, prayers, vows, pseudo-martyrdom, mad and ridiculous customs, ceremonies, observations.
          • In Pseudoprophets, visions, revelations, dreams, prophecies, new doctrines, &c., of Jews, Gentiles, Mahometans, &c.
      • Prognostics. Subsect. 4. to 3.4.1.4
        • New doctrines, paradoxes, blasphemies, madness, stupidity, despair, damnation.
      • Cures, Subsect. 5. to 3.4.1.5
        • By Physic if need be, conference, good counsel, persuasion, compulsion, correction, punishment. Quaeritur an cogi debent? Affir.
    • ♋In defect, as Memb. 2. to 3.4.2
      • Secure, void of grace and fears.
        • Epicures, Atheists, Magicians, Hypocrites, such as have cauterized consciences, or else are in a reprobate sense, worldly secure, some Philosophers, impenitent sinners. Subsect. 1. to 3.4.2.1

      • Or

        Distrustful, or too timorous, as desperate. In despair consider, Subsect. 2. to 3.4.2.2
        • His definition, Equivocations, part and parties affected. Subsect. 2. to 3.4.2.2
        • Causes Subsect. 3. to 3.4.2.3
          • The devil and his allurements, Rigid Preachers, that wound their consciences, Melancholy, contemplation, solitariness. How melancholy & despair differ. Distrust, weakness of faith. Guilty conscience for offence committed; misunderstanding Scriptures.
        • Symptoms Subsect. 4. to 3.4.2.4
          • Fear, sorrow, anguish of mind, extreme tortures and horror of conscience, fearful dreams, conceits, visions, &c.
        • Prognostics; Blasphemy, violent death. Subsect. 5. to 3.4.2.5
        • Cures Subsect. 6. to 3.4.2.6
          • Physic as occasion serves, conference, not to be idle or alone. Good counsel, good company, all comforts & contents, &c.
