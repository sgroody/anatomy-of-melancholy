From Rome: Gaius Marcius Coriolanus, who by repute was haughtily contemptuous of the plebs, and was banished in 491 BCE; Marcus Furius Camillus, a critic of populist policies for land reform, who was exiled in 391 BCE for embezzling military spoils; and Cornelius Scipio Aemilianus (Scipio Africanus the Younger, 185/4–129 BCE), another opponent of land reform, who was rumoured to have been murdered.
